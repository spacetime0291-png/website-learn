## Bab 4: Kinematika Gerak Parabola dan Melingkar

### A. Formula Utama Gerak Parabola
* $v_{0x} = v_0 \cos\alpha, \quad v_{0y} = v_0 \sin\alpha$
* Di Puncak ($v_y = 0$):
  $$t_p = \frac{v_0 \sin\alpha}{g}, \quad H_{\max} = \frac{v_0^2 \sin^2\alpha}{2g}$$
* Di Tanah ($y = 0$):
  $$t_{\text{terjauh}} = \frac{2v_0 \sin\alpha}{g}, \quad X_{\max} = \frac{v_0^2 \sin(2\alpha)}{g}$$
* Relasi Emas: $\frac{H_{\max}}{X_{\max}} = \frac{\tan\alpha}{4}$
* Tembakan Mendatar Tebing: $t = \sqrt{\frac{2h}{g}}, \quad x = v_0 \sqrt{\frac{2h}{g}}$

### B. Formula Gerak Melingkar Beraturan (GMB)
* $\omega = 2\pi f = \frac{2\pi}{T}, \quad v = \omega R$
* $1\text{ rpm} = \frac{\pi}{30}\text{ rad/s}$
* Sentripetal: $a_s = \frac{v^2}{R} = \omega^2 R, \quad F_s = \frac{m v^2}{R}$

### C. Hubungan Roda-Roda
* **Seporos:** $\omega_A = \omega_B \implies \frac{v_A}{R_A} = \frac{v_B}{R_B}$
* **Rantai / Bersinggungan:** $v_A = v_B \implies \omega_A R_A = \omega_B R_B$
