## Bab 11: Fluida Statis

### A. Tekanan Hidrostatis & Mutlak
* $P_h = \rho g h$ ($h$ = kedalaman dari permukaan air)
* $P_{\text{mutlak}} = P_0 + \rho g h$ ($P_0 \approx 10^5\text{ Pa}$)
* Pipa U: $\rho_1 h_1 = \rho_2 h_2$

### B. Hukum Pascal
* $\frac{F_1}{A_1} = \frac{F_2}{A_2} \implies F_2 = F_1 \left(\frac{D_2}{D_1}\right)^2$

### C. Hukum Archimedes
* $F_A = \rho_f V_{\text{celup}} g$
* Terapung: $\frac{V_{\text{celup}}}{V_b} = \frac{\rho_b}{\rho_f}$
* Berat Semu: $w_{\text{semu}} = w_{\text{udara}} - F_A$

### D. Gejala Kapilaritas & Stokes
* Kapilaritas: $h = \frac{2\gamma \cos\theta}{\rho g r}$
* Gaya Gesek Stokes: $F_s = 6\pi \eta r v$
