## 19. Optik Fisis (Gelombang Cahaya)

### A. Interferensi dan Difraksi
* **Interferensi Celah Ganda (Young):**
  * Terang (Konstruktif): $\frac{d \cdot p}{L} = m\lambda \quad (m = 0, 1, 2 \dots)$
  * Gelap (Destruktif): $\frac{d \cdot p}{L} = \left(m - \frac{1}{2}\right)\lambda \quad (m = 1, 2, 3 \dots)$
* **Kisi Difraksi:** $d \sin\theta = m\lambda \quad \left(d = \frac{1}{N}\right)$
* **Difraksi Celah Tunggal:**
  * Gelap: $a \sin\theta = m\lambda \quad (m = 1, 2 \dots)$
  * Terang: $a \sin\theta = \left(m + \frac{1}{2}\right)\lambda \quad (m = 1, 2 \dots)$
* **Daya Urai Kriteria Rayleigh:** $\theta_m = 1{,}22\frac{\lambda}{D}, \quad d_m = 1{,}22\frac{\lambda L}{D}$

### B. Polarisasi Cahaya
* Hukum Malus: $I_1 = \frac{1}{2}I_0, \quad I_2 = I_1 \cos^2\theta = \frac{1}{2}I_0 \cos^2\theta$
* Sudut Brewster (Polarisasi Refleksi): $\tan\theta_p = \frac{n_2}{n_1}$
