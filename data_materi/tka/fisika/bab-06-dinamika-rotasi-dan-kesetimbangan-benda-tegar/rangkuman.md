# Rangkuman Rumus Cepat — Dinamika Rotasi dan Kesetimbangan Benda Tegar
**Kategori:** TKA Fisika | **Pembungkus:** Dinamika

### A. Tabel Analogi Gerak Translasi vs Gerak Rotasi
| Besaran Fisis | Translasi (Garis Lurus) | Rotasi (Melintasi Sudut) | Hubungan Dinamika |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Inersia / Kelembaman** | Massa $m\text{ (kg)}$ | Momen Inersia $I\text{ (kg}\cdot\text{m}^2\text{)}$ | $I = \Sigma m r^2$ |
| **Penyebab Gerak** | Gaya $F = ma\text{ (N)}$ | Torsi $\tau = I\alpha\text{ (N}\cdot\text{m)}$ | $\tau = r F \sin\theta$ |
| **Momentum** | $p = mv$ | Momentum Sudut $L = I\omega$ | Kekal jika $\Sigma\tau_{\text{luar}} = 0$ |
| **Energi Kinetik** | $E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$ | $E_{k,\text{rot}} = \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2$ | $E_{k,\text{total}} = \frac{1}{2}mv^2(1 + k)$ |

### B. Tabel Momen Inersia ($I = k m R^2$) & Gerak Menggelinding
| Bentuk Benda Tegar | Faktor Inersia ($k$) | Formula Inersia ($I$) | Percepatan di Bidang Miring ($a$) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Silinder Berongga Tipis** | $k = 1$ | $I = m R^2$ | $a = \frac{1}{2} g \sin\theta$ |
| **Silinder Pejal / Cakram** | $k = 0{,}5$ | $I = \frac{1}{2} m R^2$ | $a = \frac{2}{3} g \sin\theta$ |
| **Bola Berongga Tipis** | $k \approx 0{,}67$ | $I = \frac{2}{3} m R^2$ | $a = \frac{3}{5} g \sin\theta$ |
| **Bola Pejal** | $k = 0{,}4$ | $I = \frac{2}{5} m R^2$ | $a = \frac{5}{7} g \sin\theta$ |
