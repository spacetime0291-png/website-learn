# Rangkuman Rumus Cepat — Termodinamika
**Kategori:** TKA Fisika | **Pembungkus:** Kalor dan Termodinamika

### A. Tabel 4 Proses Termodinamika Gas
| Proses Termal | Karakteristik Keadaan | Usaha Luar Gas ($W$) | Perubahan Energi Dalam ($\Delta U$) | Kalor Diserap/Dilepas ($Q$) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Isobarik** | $P = \text{konstan}$ | $W = P(V_2 - V_1) = nR\Delta T$ | $\Delta U = \frac{f}{2}nR\Delta T$ | $Q = \Delta U + W$ |
| **Isokhorik** | $V = \text{konstan} \implies \Delta V = 0$ | $W = 0$ | $\Delta U = Q = \frac{f}{2}nR\Delta T$ | $Q = \Delta U$ |
| **Isotermal** | $T = \text{konstan} \implies \Delta T = 0$ | $W = nRT \ln\left(\frac{V_2}{V_1}\right)$ | $\Delta U = 0$ | $Q = W$ |
| **Adiabatik** | $Q = 0$ (tanpa aliran kalor) | $W = -\Delta U = \frac{P_1 V_1 - P_2 V_2}{\gamma - 1}$ | $\Delta U = -W$ | $P_1 V_1^\gamma = P_2 V_2^\gamma$ |

### B. Tabel Mesin Kalor Carnot & Refrigerator
| Jenis Siklus Mesin | Formulasi Efisiensi / Kinerja | Hubungan Energi |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mesin Kalor Carnot** | $\eta = 1 - \frac{T_c}{T_h} = \frac{W}{Q_h} = \frac{Q_h - Q_c}{Q_h}$ | Efisiensi teoretis maksimum antara dua reservoir |
| **Refrigerator (Mesin Pendingin)** | $COP = \frac{Q_c}{W} = \frac{T_c}{T_h - T_c}$ | $W = Q_h - Q_c$ |
| **Heat Pump (Pompa Kalor)** | $COP_{\text{HP}} = \frac{Q_h}{W} = \frac{T_h}{T_h - T_c} = COP + 1$ | Memindahkan kalor ke ruang bersuhu tinggi |
