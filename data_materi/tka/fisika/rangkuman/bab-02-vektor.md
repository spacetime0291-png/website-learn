## Bab 2: Vektor

### A. Rumus Resultan Dua Vektor
* **Penjumlahan:** $R = \sqrt{F_1^2 + F_2^2 + 2F_1 F_2 \cos\theta}$
* **Selisih:** $S = \sqrt{F_1^2 + F_2^2 - 2F_1 F_2 \cos\theta}$
* **Syarat $R = S$:** Hanya saat $\theta = 90^\circ$ ($R = S = \sqrt{F_1^2 + F_2^2}$).

### B. Nilai Cepat Dua Vektor Sama Besar ($F_1 = F_2 = F$)
* $\theta = 0^\circ \implies R = 2F$
* $\theta = 60^\circ \implies R = F\sqrt{3}$
* $\theta = 90^\circ \implies R = F\sqrt{2}$
* $\theta = 120^\circ \implies R = F$
* $\theta = 180^\circ \implies R = 0$

### C. Penguraian Vektor & Metode Analitik
* $F_x = F \cos\theta$, $F_y = F \sin\theta$ (jika $\theta$ terhadap sumbu-$x$).
* Resultan Banyak Vektor:
  $$R = \sqrt{(\sum F_x)^2 + (\sum F_y)^2}, \quad \tan\alpha = \frac{\sum F_y}{\sum F_x}$$

### D. Perkalian Titik vs Silang
* **Dot Product:** $\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = AB\cos\theta = A_x B_x + A_y B_y + A_z B_z$ (Skalar).
  * $\vec{A} \perp \vec{B} \iff \vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = 0$.
* **Cross Product:** $|\vec{A} \times \vec{B}| = AB\sin\theta$ (Vektor tegak lurus bidang).
  * $\hat{i} \times \hat{j} = \hat{k}$, $\hat{j} \times \hat{k} = \hat{i}$, $\hat{k} \times \hat{i} = \hat{j}$.
  * $\vec{A} \parallel \vec{B} \iff \vec{A} \times \vec{B} = 0$.
