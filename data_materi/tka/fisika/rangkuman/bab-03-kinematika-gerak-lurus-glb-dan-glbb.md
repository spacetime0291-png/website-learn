## Bab 3: Kinematika Gerak Lurus (GLB dan GLBB)

### A. Formula GLB ($a = 0$)
* $s = v \cdot t$
* Papasan: $t = \frac{S_{\text{tot}}}{v_A + v_B}$
* Susulan: $t = \frac{s_0}{v_B - v_A}$

### B. Formula Utama GLBB ($a = \text{konstan}$)
1. $v_t = v_0 + at$
2. $s = v_0 t + \frac{1}{2}at^2$
3. $v_t^2 = v_0^2 + 2as$
4. $s = \left(\frac{v_0 + v_t}{2}\right)t$

### C. Ringkasan Gerak Vertikal ($g \approx 10\text{ m/s}^2$)
* **Jatuh Bebas ($v_0 = 0$):**
  $$v_t = \sqrt{2gh}, \quad h = \frac{1}{2}gt^2, \quad t = \sqrt{\frac{2h}{g}}$$
* **Vertikal ke Atas:**
  $$t_{\text{puncak}} = \frac{v_0}{g}, \quad h_{\max} = \frac{v_0^2}{2g}, \quad t_{\text{total}} = \frac{2v_0}{g}$$
